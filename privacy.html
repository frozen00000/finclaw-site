FinClaw

Privacy policy

Effective October 19, 2026.

FinClaw is a personal, self-hosted finance dashboard. It runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by that owner. This policy explains what data FinClaw handles and how.

Data FinClaw collects

  • Account details: account names, types, masked account numbers and institution names.
  • Balances and holdings for linked accounts.
  • Transactions: dates, amounts, descriptions, merchant names and categories.
  • Access tokens issued by Plaid and SnapTrade, which let FinClaw read the linked accounts.

FinClaw never receives or stores bank usernames or passwords. You sign in to your financial institution directly in Plaid's or SnapTrade's secure window.

How data is used

Data is used only to show the owner their own net worth, portfolio performance, income, spending and transfers. FinClaw does not use data for advertising, does not build profiles for third parties, and does not sell, rent or share data with anyone.

Where data is stored

Data is stored on the owner's own computer, in a local database that is not reachable from the internet. Access tokens are kept only in that database and are never sent anywhere except to the provider that issued them.

Third-party services

FinClaw uses these services to connect to financial institutions:

  • Plaid for bank and credit card accounts.
  • SnapTrade for brokerage accounts.

Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policies, linked above.

Retention and deletion

Disconnecting an account in FinClaw revokes its access token with the provider, and FinClaw stops receiving data from it. All locally stored data can be deleted at any time by removing the local database.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
