FinClaw

All your accounts, one private dashboard.

FinClaw is a personal finance app that brings investment, bank and credit card accounts together, so you can see your net worth, portfolio performance and cash flow in one place.

Investments

Holdings and performance across brokerage accounts, compared with market benchmarks.

Banking & cash flow

Income, spending and transfers across checking, savings and credit cards.

Private by design

Runs on its owner's own computer. Financial data is never sold or shared.

How accounts are connected

Bank and credit card accounts are linked through Plaid, and brokerage accounts through SnapTrade. You sign in to your bank in their secure window; FinClaw never sees or stores your bank username or password. You can disconnect an account at any time, which revokes FinClaw's access to it.

FinClaw is a personal, self-hosted project. It is not offered as a public service and has no sign-up.
